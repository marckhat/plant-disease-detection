What is Grad-CAM?

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping highlights the spatial regions in the input image that most influenced the model's prediction. Warm colors indicate high relevance.

Reading the map
Bright regions
Lesions, spots, or visual cues the model used.
Dark regions
Areas the model effectively ignored.
Why it matters

A correct prediction backed by a heatmap that focuses on the lesion is trustworthy. A correct prediction with a heatmap focused on the background is a warning sign.

Why fine-tuning on PlantDoc matters

PlantVillage images are taken in controlled lab conditions — flat backgrounds, perfect lighting. A model trained only on PlantVillage looks great on PlantVillage's own test set but fails on real field photos. Fine-tuning on PlantDoc bridges that domain gap.

03
Fine-tune joint_aug
+ PlantDoc · + augmented PlantDoc.
04
Fine-tune joint_all
Focal Loss · CutMix · frozen→unfrozen.
+45 percentage points on real-world test accuracy from step 02 to step 04. The augmented PlantDoc data alone accounts for ~15 points of that.
Hard classes
Corn Gray Leaf Spot, Potato Early/Late Blight, and Tomato Bacterial Spot are visually similar to neighbouring classes and have F1 below 0.45.
Out-of-distribution
Diseases outside the 27-class taxonomy will be silently misclassified into the nearest visible class. The model does not return an "unknown" option.
Edge cases
Extreme lighting, heavy motion blur, occluded leaves, and multi-leaf photographs degrade accuracy. Confidence below ~50% should be treated as inconclusive.
